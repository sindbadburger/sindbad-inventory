# Sindbad Inventory — Staff SOP
*Standard Operating Procedure for placing inventory orders*

---

## What This App Is

A digital ordering tool. When the restaurant is running low on something, you tap the item, add it to your cart, and submit. The manager receives the order immediately by email and it's logged to a Google Sheet.

**Don't have it on your phone yet?** Read the **Install Guide** first.

---

## When to Use It

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| You notice we're low on something during your shift | Add it to the cart and submit at end of shift |
| Manager asks for a full inventory check | Walk the kitchen + storage with the app open, tap every item that's running low, submit |
| End of week / scheduled inventory day | Open the app and review by category |

> 🟢 You can submit **as many orders as you need** during a shift. Smaller, more frequent orders are better than one giant end-of-week order.

---

## Step-by-Step

### 1. Open the app
Tap the **Sindbad Inventory** icon on your home screen.

### 2. Enter PIN
The PIN is **1981**. Same for all staff.

### 3. Enter your name
Type your first name (e.g. `Ali`).

### 4. Confirm the date
Today's date is filled in automatically.

### 5. Browse items by category
At the top of the screen are tabs:
- **Misc**
- **Cleaning**
- **Proteins**
- **Produce**
- **Pickles & Sauces**
- **Spices & Dry**
- **Dairy & Cheese**
- **Frozen & Fry**
- **Bread & Wraps**

Tap a tab to see all the items in that category.
Or use the **search bar** at the top to find an item by name.

### 6. Add items to your cart
Tap the item card to add it to your cart. The item is now in your order.

To **add a note** (e.g. *"need 2 cases not 1"*, *"urgent — out by tomorrow"*, *"prefer Sysco brand"*), tap the note icon on the item.

### 7. Review your cart
Tap the **cart icon** to open your order drawer.
Check that everything looks right.
You can **remove an item** by tapping the X if you added it by mistake.

### 8. Submit the order
Tap the **Submit Order** button.
The order is sent to the manager immediately.

### 9. Done
You'll see a success screen confirming the order was sent.

## Tips for a Good Order

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Add notes when the standard quantity is wrong | Order things we already have plenty of |
| Mark urgent items in the notes | Order items you're unsure about — ask the manager first |
| Submit at end of shift, not the middle | Send 10 separate orders for one shift |
| Check the cart drawer before submitting | Forget to specify quantity if it differs from normal |
| Tell the manager verbally if it's truly urgent | Assume the app delivers physical product — the SUPPLIER does |

## After You Submit

Behind the scenes:
- A row is added to the inventory Google Sheet.
- A branded email is sent to **Info@sindbadburger.com** with your order.
- The manager places the actual supplier order based on your submission.

If something is urgent (we'll run out before tomorrow's order arrives), **also tell the manager directly** — don't rely on email.

---

## Common Situations

**"I see two items that mean the same thing. Which do I add?"**
Pick either — the manager will deduplicate. Don't add both.

**"The item I need isn't in the list."**
Add a similar item with a note explaining what you actually need. Or text the manager.

**"I accidentally submitted the wrong items."**
Just submit a correction order — the manager sees both and uses the latest.

**"The app froze."**
Pull down on the page to refresh, or close the app and reopen. Your cart is lost when you do this, so add items again.
